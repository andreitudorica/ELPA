## Publication safety, by layer

**Database constraints** (probed inside a rolled-back transaction; every
forbidden write was refused with a check violation):

1. Machine-policy decision with outcome `verified` and no eligibility.
2. Machine-policy `verified` decision for the high-consequence category.
3. Administrator decision with a reason shorter than ten characters.
4. Machine-policy `rejected` decision.
5. Decision with any publication value other than `separate_decision_required`.
6. Eligibility with a single reviewer.
7. Eligibility for the high-consequence category.
8. Eligibility with a false-approval upper bound above 5 %.

**Write boundary** (`verification-safety.test.ts`): batch writes refuse
machine verification without an active eligibility of the same category and
policy version, refuse revoked or mismatched eligibility, refuse the
high-consequence category even with an eligibility id, refuse Administrator or
rejection outcomes from the machine path, and audit every batch inside its
transaction. Overrides need an Administrator method, a reason, a supported
outcome and a decision not already superseded, and are audited.

**Decision engine** (`decision.test.ts`): recommendations never become
`verified` without an approved eligibility and calibrated threshold; the
high-consequence category is never autonomous; an assessment can block but cannot
bypass validation; superseded Claims are not eligible; conflicting Claims are
contradictions; follow-up applies only in the selective strategy.

**Assessor boundary** (`assessor.test.ts`): only allowlisted passages are sent
under opaque references; invented references, quotes absent from the passage,
support without citation, schema violations, refusals and captures for a
different request are rejected.

**Module boundaries:** the pure policy code cannot import NestJS, Drizzle,
repositories, schemas or model SDKs (ESLint, probed); the model adapter returns
captures and has no persistence dependency; Curation reads Research through its
barrel; Research and Catalog tables are never written by Curation.

**HTTP boundary:** every verification route is mounted by `@AdminController`
under `/api/admin/verification` (asserted in tests and by the boot-time route
verifier). The anonymous Recommendation module has no controllers and imports no
Research or Curation module, so no public API exposes Evidence, Claims or
decisions.

**Licensing:** decisions carry their source's publication constraints (register
reuse terms pending legal review, ODbL attribution and share-alike, CDLA
attribution); Data Studio shows them as reasons publication is blocked. Image
Evidence is never sent to a model.

## Known limits

- The eligibility-to-decision category match is enforced by the write boundary,
  not by a cross-table database constraint.
- There is still no API-plus-database integration test tier; database constraints
  were verified by a probe, not by an automated test.
- No browser check of the Data Studio panel was performed; it is covered by
  typechecking, lint and component tests.
- The model captures are not reproducible bit for bit; reproduction reuses the
  captured file.
